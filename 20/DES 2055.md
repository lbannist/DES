
In this first module of the course, students expand their understanding to CAD.  The following challenges are done using Fusion360

They learn about
- Gridfinity Tool Tray
- Knurlings
- Sheet Metal - Steel
- Sheet Metal - Cardboard Box
- Sheet Metal - Bumper
- Joints
- Joints - Threaded
- Geneva Mechanism

After completing this series of challenges, they then decide which branch of Design they would like to focus on.

2D Animation - Krita
3D Animation - Blender
