# Animation 30 – Cross-Technique Fusion (2D + Effects/Compositing)

## Unit I: World & Mood Building — Challenge I3 (Unit Capstone)

**Time:** 1 Week (4–5 Class Periods)
**Due Date:** Following Monday before class

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this assignment students will:

- Animate a character reacting convincingly to an environmental effect
- Apply basic compositing to unify character and background lighting/color
- Justify why an effect enhances the story rather than existing for its own sake
- Combine skills from I1 (environment) and I2 (mood/color) into one integrated scene

Students will learn that effects and compositing are storytelling tools, not decoration layered on top of a finished scene.

---

## 💭 Production Question

### Does the effect serve the story, or is it just there to look cool?

This is the Unit I capstone. Reuse your I1 environment, apply a mood treatment from I2, and now add a character who convincingly reacts to an environmental effect within that world.

---

## Checkpoint 1 — Research and Analysis (Day 1)

**📖 Effect Integration Study**

Watch 2–3 scenes where a character convincingly reacts to weather or environmental effects (rain, wind, fire, snow). Answer:

1. How does the character's body language change because of the effect?
2. How does the effect affect the character's lighting or color?
3. What would be lost if the character simply stood in the scene without reacting?
4. How is the effect timed relative to the character's motion?

---

## Checkpoint 2 — Planning (Day 1–2)

**✏️ Choose Your Effect and Reaction**

Using your I1 environment (with a I2 color treatment if you choose), select one environmental effect (rain, wind, fire nearby, snow) and decide how your character reacts to it.

**Plan**

- Sketch your character's key reaction poses
- Note how the effect will influence the character's lighting/color to feel unified with the scene
- Identify the timing relationship — does the reaction happen before, during, or after the effect intensifies?

---

## Checkpoint 3 — Animation Production (Days 2–4)

**✏️ Build your integrated scene in Krita, using your plan from Checkpoint 2 as your guide.**

**Document Settings**

- 1920 × 1080
- 12 FPS

**Requirements**

- 6–10 second animation
- One environmental effect looping or progressing smoothly
- Character reaction that is clearly caused by the effect (not incidental)
- Character lighting/color adjusted to feel unified with the environment (basic compositing)
- Built on or adapted from your I1 environment

---

## Checkpoint 4 — Reflection (Day 5)

**✏️ Answer:**

1. How does the effect enhance your story, specifically?
2. What compositing adjustment made the biggest difference in unifying character and background?
3. What would this scene lose if the character didn't react to the effect at all?
4. How did your I1 environment and I2 color choices carry into this piece?

## 📊 Assessment Criteria

**Effect & Reaction Integration (40%)**

- Character reaction is clearly and convincingly caused by the effect
- Effect enhances rather than distracts from the story

**Compositing (30%)**

- Character lighting/color unified with the environment
- Effect and character interact believably (occlusion, shadow, etc. where relevant)

**Technical Execution (20%)**

- Smooth, looping or progressing effect
- Consistent with I1/I2 environment work

**Reflection (10%)**

- Clear justification of why the effect serves the story
